Read as a Lattice

Tyranny is not a foreign species of arrangement that descends on a society from outside. It is what happens when four mechanisms that are always running — material control, trait selection, information flow, and the emotional-moral cost of seeing clearly — stop operating loosely and lock into a single, self-reinforcing loop. How this reads

Each node drives the one downstream of it. The dashed centre-lines are the lattice's cross-links — the part that makes it a coupled system rather than four separate stories: concentration doesn't just select traits, it also reaches straight across to reshape what people can feel is true.

The faint outward lines marked RUPTURE? and FRACTURE? are deliberately unresolved — elite coalition breakdown, external shock, economic collapse. This model describes the lock-in well. It doesn't yet describe what breaks it.

Reading note — this is a descriptive model of how coercive systems self-reinforce, built by combining ordinary feedback-loop thinking with concepts already named in the literature (moral disengagement, moral injury, elite-coalition theory). It is not a physical law, and the loop drawn here is a claim to be tested against real cases, not a proof.
